# Squares in Squares milestone review

Project ID: `squares-in-squares`. Follow the [manual procedure](../REVIEW.md) and
[project policy](POLICY.md). Owner-manual initiation only; no automatic or scheduled
Claude reviews. Use the shared 15-point budget gate before spending this project's
4-point allocation or owner-assigned contingency.

Select a review after the geometry/metric contract is fixed, serialized placements have
independent validity evidence, or a material search method changes. The owner separately
authorized this offline baseline implementation; these milestones do not initiate a
Claude review.

Ask Claude to check unit-square geometry, rotations/radians, non-overlap and interval or
precision issues, decimal round-trip validity, official metric version/score aggregation,
partial-puzzle handling, deterministic seeds/checkpoints and external-record provenance.
Use synthetic geometric cases and aggregate evidence rather than private packing artifacts.

Bind the review to `squares-in-squares`, base/head SHA and full experiment IDs. Keep
sanitized findings and owner decisions in `projects/squares-in-squares/reviews/`; do not
attach LLM records, private artifacts or author chat to a fresh blind review.
